# Which one worked? Legendeering rounds (v9)

Built 2026-09-10 on the v8 engine after Evan's correction that Talex does not make commercials, then rebuilt the same evening after his second correction: no round may show a "No film to show" poster, and the comparison he likes best is one company two ways. Four rounds, each a single company's show against that company's own commercial, with a real film on both sides. Prototype, draft for Tom.

## What it is

The landing page is deliberately short: the gate quote from Tom's "What's 100 Years Old is New Again" and the four rounds as a list, each a link (`#r1` to `#r4`) that opens that round's game. A round is four screens, the same as the live case study: pick one of two cards, see the podium (gold and silver) with your pick beside the real one, answer one "why did it work?" question, read the source and the takeaway. Two points a round, eight in all, with the points pill, streak, tier and confetti carried over from v8. Every film plays muted inside the card.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The page and the engine. Hash routes, one render path per screen. Embeds play muted. The engine refuses a side without a film. |
| `content.js` | Every word. `SIDES` (eight sides, two per round), `ROUNDS`, `COPY`. Regenerated by the build. |
| `build-content.js` | Copies the two library sides (Rico's Tacos, Second Act) byte for byte from `format-blocks.json`, writes the authored sides and rounds, checks that every side has an eleven-character YouTube id with a label and caption, checks that the result copy matches the round count, and stamps a content hash onto the script URL in `index.html`. Run it after any copy change. |

## Parked

Dropped from the first cut because no honest same-company film pair exists: YouTube creators against a Netflix hit (concepts, not two films from one company), Michelin's guide against tire ads, HubSpot's plain answers against keyword stuffing, Polaroid's billboards against machine-made content. Dropped after sourcing: GoPro Awards Top 10 (`3bRgp_GSyBQ`, 4.45M views) against the HERO6 launch spot (`vr0qNXmkUJ8`, 17.9M views), because the commercial has four times the views of the awards film and the round could not honestly call the awards film "the one that worked" on any measure a prospect would accept.

## Rules kept

Quotes verbatim from the source named on each round. The "why" answers are prepared by Talex and labelled not yet approved by Tom. No commercial appears as a Talex example; a commercial appears only as the thing the show beat, and always from the same company. Same palette, fonts and logo as the strategy template.
